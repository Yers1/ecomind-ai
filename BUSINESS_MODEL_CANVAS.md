# EcoMind AI — business model canvas notes

This document records mentor feedback as a proposed operating model. Named companies are prospective ecosystem participants, not current partners, customers or endorsers.

## Key partners

- Marketplaces and retailers: prospective relationships with Amazon, ASOS, Zalando (mentor message said “Zealand”; confirm the intended company) and other shopping destinations.
- Commerce platforms: prospective integrations with Shopify, Shopee and BigCommerce.
- Certification providers: authoritative certification bodies and data providers that can supply names, criteria, identifiers, verification links and permitted data feeds.
- Hosting, domain, security and privacy suppliers.

No partnership is currently claimed. A name can move from “prospective” to “partner” only after a signed agreement and approved public wording.

## Key resources

- Tech Lead: extension, web app, data architecture, security, scoring implementation and platform reliability.
- Partnership Lead: marketplace, commerce-platform, retailer and certification-provider relationships and accounts.
- Product/design, sustainability-methodology expertise, legal/privacy support and customer research.
- Versioned certification registry, evidence provenance, parser fixtures and permissioned platform feeds.

## Key activities

- Maintain the local user-activated browser-extension analysis and transparent scoring methodology.
- Phase 1: build best-effort adapters for information visible on the active product page, using user activation, minimum permissions and sanitised fixtures. Do not mass-scrape, bypass access controls or claim universal compatibility.
- Phase 2: replace fragile page extraction with documented APIs or licensed feeds after partnership and data-use agreements are signed.
- Validate environmental factors and certification eligibility with independent experts.
- Maintain partner accounts, data-quality monitoring, privacy controls and support.

## Cost structure

- Staff and contractor costs, including technical maintenance and partnership management.
- Hosting, database, authentication, monitoring, security and backup fees, including equivalent in-house infrastructure costs.
- Domain registration and renewal; `EcoMind.org` is a naming idea only and availability/trademark clearance must be checked before purchase.
- Chrome Web Store, legal, privacy, insurance, certification-data licensing, accessibility testing and independent methodology review.
- Partner integration, API usage, data cleansing and ongoing parser/feed maintenance.

## Evidence and partnership guardrails

- Retailer policy must not be presented as evidence for a specific product unless applicability is clear.
- A certification provider relationship does not make every seller claim verified.
- Social/labour evidence remains separate from environmental scoring unless documented environmental criteria are explicitly adopted.
- Product and browsing data are not public leaderboard fields.
