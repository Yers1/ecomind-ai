# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young people aged 15–24 who shop across multiple online retailers and need a clear, low-pressure way to understand incomplete environmental product information while making a purchase decision. The primary workflow is a user-activated browser-extension analysis on the current clothing product page, followed by evidence review, optional correction and cross-retailer comparison. Threadly remains the reliable offline presentation fixture.

## Product Purpose

EcoMind AI is a privacy-conscious multi-retailer educational decision-support tool. It extracts visible and structured product evidence locally after activation, separates page evidence, user-provided values, estimates and missing data, then applies a published deterministic prototype formula only when clothing evidence is sufficient. Success means users can analyse and compare real clothing pages without universal-retailer claims, certification claims, verified savings or a server-side scraper.

## Brand Personality

Supportive, transparent and quietly optimistic. The product should feel credible enough for a climate-technology demonstration, friendly enough for younger shoppers and calm enough to explain uncertainty without judgement.

## Anti-references

EcoMind must not resemble greenwashing, an Amazon-only product, an official retailer integration, a scientific certification portal, a points system that rewards consumption, or a developer test harness. Avoid fake compatibility claims, fake alternatives, unexplained AI scores, hidden uncertainty, duplicated actions, inflated sustainability claims and generic eco decoration that obscures the shopping task.

## Design Principles

1. Reveal evidence before persuasion: show the extracted listing information, missing fields and source type before encouraging an alternative.
2. Make uncertainty legible: distinguish confirmed zero from unknown data and label provisional results without false precision.
3. Keep user agency explicit: analyse only after activation and reward learning, saving, repair/reuse or mindful non-purchase—not an implied purchase.
4. Preserve the shopping context: retailer-specific parsers enrich shared structured-data fallbacks, while the koala remains visibly separate as an extension layer.
5. Explain the system: AI structures information; the published deterministic formula calculates the score.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All functionality must work by keyboard, focus must move into and return from dialogs, Escape must close active dialogs, background content must be inert while a modal is open, status must not rely on colour alone, reduced motion must be respected and the drawer must remain usable at 390 × 844.
